# Keyword demand search, thepoojaedit.in and studio.thepoojaedit.in (2 Nov 2026)

Read-only research in the Pooja profile's Google Ads Keyword Planner (account 897-136-1933). No campaigns, settings or spend. Nothing on the site was edited. Data files: `docs/keyword-demand-2026-11-02/set1-4.csv`.

## Method and limits
- India, English, Google, Oct 2025 to Sep 2026, "Exclude adult ideas" filter on. Volumes are Google's buckets (10-100, 100-1K, 1K-10K, 10K-100K), never exact.
- Four seed sets, 5 seeds each:
  1. Core: personal stylist, personal shopper, fashion stylist, wardrobe consultant, trousseau shopping (1,103 ideas).
  2. Questions and cost: personal stylist cost, how much does a stylist cost, personal shopper charges, reel editing price, how to become a ugc creator (71 ideas, all read).
  3. Location and audience: personal stylist delhi, personal shopper gurgaon, nri shopping india, buy indian clothes from abroad, wedding shopping delhi (534 ideas).
  4. Adjacent (studio): reel editor, instagram reel editing, ugc creator, video editor for instagram, youtube video editor (2,771 ideas).
- **Deviation:** the "Download keyword ideas .csv" click showed "Downloading report" but no file ever reached ~/Downloads (searched the whole home folder). I read the on-screen table instead, sorted by volume, highest first. So sets 1, 3 and 4 cover the top of the list (every 1K+ idea and most of the 100-1K ideas); the long tail of 10-100 and below was not captured. Set 2 was read in full.
- Filters applied in analysis (python3 -I): deduped across sets; dropped names (celebrity stylists), apps and software searches, "meaning" definitions, rental and wedding-card terms, men's-only terms and other cities; kept 100+ a month. 75 phrases kept out of 138 unique.
- Search Console comparison uses 19-31 Oct only (13 days; that is all the property holds): 10 clicks, 350 impressions, avg position 7.3.

## Gaps
1. No page for "wedding shopping in Delhi" for people who live in Delhi (topic 4). Demand: 1K-10K for the Chandni Chowk lehenga searches and 100-1K for about 20 related phrases.
2. No short definition for "what is a ugc creator" (topic 1), the biggest bucket we touch.
3. No explicit "reel editor" wording tested in the /reels title (topic 3).
