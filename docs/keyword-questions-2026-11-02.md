# Questions people actually ask, with our answer plan (2 Nov 2026)

Source: Google Ads Keyword Planner (India, Oct 2025 to Sep 2026, volume buckets only) plus our own Search Console queries (19-31 Oct, real searches that showed our pages). Question-style seeds were searched directly, because the "keyword text contains" filter would not apply reliably in the Ads UI. Wedding-shopping questions came from a filtered run of the "location and audience" set (116 of 534 ideas were question or buying-intent phrases). Nothing was edited on the site.
